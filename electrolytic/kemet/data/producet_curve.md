# Series summary 
## A series (<A780)
polymer aluminum capacitor  
surface mount, low voltage (<=63V), low capacitance, low high frequency ESR, high ripple, normal lifetime  

## hybrid series (>A780 for surface mount, PHA for axial/radial)
hybrid polymer aluminum capacitor, similar with polymer
low voltage (<=63V), relative low capacitance, relative low high frequency ESR, normal lifetime, high ripple  

## high ripple aluminum (PE,AL,AAR)
aluminum
low to high voltage, large cap, low ripple,normal life  

## low ripple aluminum (EL,ES,ED)
aluminum
low to high voltage, low cap, low ripple, represented by tan_delta
